First-hackathon checklist

This week

  • Lock your team of 2 to 4 and decide who builds, who designs and who presents
  • Register, and read the FAQ on the official site
  • Set up your laptop: editor, Git, Node or Python, and a starter template you've used before
  • Decide who connects the frontend and the backend, and write their name down

Pack

  • Laptop, charger and an extension board
  • Student ID (you won't get in without it)
  • Phone charger or power bank, earphones
  • Water bottle, a hoodie, basic toiletries
  • A notebook for the idea and the pitch

At the venue

  • Read every problem statement and sponsor track before you pick
  • Scope one user and one flow you can demo
  • Freeze features well before the deadline and fix only what judges will see
  • Record a backup demo video on a laptop and a phone
  • Rehearse a 3-minute pitch with a timer: use my template

After

  • Push the code with a README and the demo video
  • Post what you built and tag your teammates
  • Connect with the mentors and judges you spoke to
